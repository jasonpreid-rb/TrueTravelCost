The Toyota Corolla Hybrid has the lowest true cost: €179.85 for the journey, or €0.36 per km.

Why each method was chosen

Fuel or energy. Uses real-world consumption rather than the official test figure, because motorway driving with a full load uses more energy than the laboratory cycle.

Maintenance and tyres. Both wear with distance, so they are charged per kilometre driven rather than per year.

Depreciation. Only the value lost through mileage is charged to the journey. Value lost through age alone would be lost whether or not the trip was made, so it is left out.

Tolls. Taken from the published tariff for the route and vehicle class. If no toll is known for a route, it is treated as €0.00.

Methodology and limitationsMunich to Milan · 493 km

Data sources

In a live report each input is tagged with where it came from and the date it was retrieved.

InputSource
Vehicle specificationVehicle database entry for the chosen make, model, engine and trim
ConsumptionManufacturer figures adjusted to real-world motorway driving
Fuel and electricity pricesAverage prices for the route's country on the price date
Tolls and chargesPublished tariffs for the route and vehicle class
Maintenance, tyres and depreciationTypical per-kilometre rates for the vehicle type and age
DistanceRouting service, fastest driving route

Limitations

  • Fuel consumption changes with traffic, weather, speed, load and driving style.
  • Maintenance and tyre costs vary by garage, region and how the vehicle is driven.
  • Future depreciation cannot be known with certainty. The report uses an estimate of value lost through mileage.
  • Insurance, road tax, financing and parking are not part of this report.
  • Prices change. Figures are correct for the price date shown on page 3.
Estimates are not guarantees. This report is an estimate based on the information and assumptions stated in it. It is provided for information only and is not financial advice. Full methodology: truetravelcost.com/methodology
