Example Comprehensive Report

What if you could see the complete estimated cost of a journey rather than just the fuel bill?

A comprehensive TrueTravelCost report brings the journey, vehicle and estimated costs together in one detailed report.

The report below is an example using fictional figures to demonstrate the type of information that can be presented.

Journey Overview

Route: Munich → Milan

Example vehicle: BMW 320d

Example distance: 493 km

Journey type: One-way

All figures on this page are illustrative.

Estimated True Travel Cost

Cost Estimated amount
Fuel €52.40
Tolls €48.00
Maintenance €31.60
Tyres €12.80
Depreciation / vehicle wear €67.50
Total estimated journey cost €212.30

Estimated cost per kilometre: €0.43

Fuel Cost vs True Travel Cost

The example journey has an estimated fuel cost of €52.40.

Looking only at fuel would therefore suggest that the journey costs around €52.

When the other estimated costs are considered, the total rises to approximately €212.

This illustrates the central idea behind TrueTravelCost: the cost of travelling isn't necessarily the same as the cost of fuel.

Cost Breakdown

Fuel — €52.40

The estimated fuel requirement for the journey based on the example vehicle's consumption and assumed fuel price.

Tolls — €48.00

Example route-related toll costs.

Maintenance — €31.60

An estimated allocation of vehicle maintenance costs based on kilometres travelled.

Tyres — €12.80

An estimated contribution towards tyre wear and replacement.

Depreciation and vehicle wear — €67.50

An illustrative estimate representing the effect of vehicle use and mileage on the broader cost of ownership.

What if this wasn't a one-off trip?

The same journey can look very different when repeated regularly.

Imagine making a similar 493-kilometre journey every month.

At an illustrative €212.30 per journey, twelve journeys would represent approximately €2,547.60 of annual travel cost.

The actual result would depend on the vehicle, route, prices and frequency of travel.

What a comprehensive report could include

A full report can bring together significantly more information than a simple journey calculation.

  • Journey overview
  • Route and distance
  • Vehicle information
  • Fuel or energy consumption
  • Fuel or electricity cost
  • Tolls and journey charges
  • Maintenance estimates
  • Tyre costs
  • Vehicle wear
  • Depreciation estimates
  • Total estimated travel cost
  • Cost per kilometre
  • Round-trip calculations
  • Annualised costs for regular journeys
  • Calculation assumptions
  • Methodology and data information

Useful when you're making a decision

A detailed travel cost report isn't only useful after you've decided where to drive.

It can also help when you're comparing vehicles, considering a new commute or trying to understand the financial impact of regular driving.

Important: estimates are not guarantees

Vehicle costs vary from driver to driver and from vehicle to vehicle.

Fuel consumption can change with traffic, weather, speed and driving style. Maintenance and tyre costs vary. Future depreciation cannot be known with certainty.

A TrueTravelCost report therefore represents an estimate based on the available information and stated assumptions.
