One-off trips and daily commutes

Not every journey is the same.

You might drive hundreds of kilometres for a holiday once a year, or make the same 40-kilometre journey every working day.

TrueTravelCost helps you understand the cost of both.

Calculate the cost of a one-off trip

Planning a road trip, visiting family, travelling to an airport or simply driving somewhere for the day?

A fuel calculator can tell you approximately how much fuel you'll use. But the fuel bill isn't necessarily the complete cost of making the journey.

Depending on the vehicle and route, the wider cost can include fuel, maintenance, tyres, wear, depreciation and tolls.

TrueTravelCost brings these factors together to provide an estimated overall journey cost.

What does your commute really cost?

Daily commuting is where small costs can become much more significant.

Imagine your journey costs an estimated €18 each way.

One journey doesn't necessarily sound significant. But if that journey is repeated every working day, the annual figure becomes much larger.

For example:

  • €18 per journey
  • €36 per day for a return trip
  • €180 per five-day working week
  • €3,960 over 220 working days

The exact cost will depend on your vehicle, route, prices and assumptions, but the principle is important: frequency matters.

Fuel isn't the whole story

If your car uses 7 litres of fuel per 100 kilometres, that tells you something important about its energy consumption.

It doesn't tell you everything about what those 100 kilometres cost.

Every kilometre also contributes to the use of tyres, maintenance and other vehicle-related costs. The vehicle may also lose value as mileage increases.

That's the reason behind TrueTravelCost: to look beyond the fuel pump and consider the broader cost of travelling by car.
