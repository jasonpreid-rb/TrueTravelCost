How it works

How TrueTravelCost works

Most journey calculators answer a simple question: how much fuel will I use? TrueTravelCost asks a bigger one: what does this journey actually cost me?

Calculate your true travel cost

Fuel is only one part of the cost

Fuel is only one part of the cost of using a vehicle. TrueTravelCost brings together several vehicle and journey costs to produce a broader estimate.

Step 1: Enter your journey

Start by entering where you're travelling from and where you're going.

The journey distance provides the foundation for the calculation, because many vehicle costs increase as kilometres are driven.

A short local journey and a 1,000-kilometre road trip can therefore produce very different results.

Step 2: Enter your vehicle

Different vehicles have different costs.

Fuel consumption, maintenance requirements, tyre costs, depreciation and other factors can vary significantly between vehicles.

That's why TrueTravelCost is designed to calculate the journey in the context of the vehicle being used.

Step 3: Calculate more than fuel

The calculation can consider several components of vehicle and journey cost.

01 / ENERGY

Fuel or electricity

The estimated energy required for the journey based on the vehicle and relevant energy-price assumptions.

02 / MAINTENANCE

Maintenance

Vehicles require servicing and maintenance as they accumulate mileage. An estimated cost can be allocated across the kilometres travelled.

03 / TYRES

Tyres

Tyres wear as they are used. Their expected cost and lifespan can therefore contribute to the cost of travelling.

04 / WEAR

Vehicle wear

Driving contributes to wear on components and systems. This can be considered as part of the broader cost of vehicle use.

05 / VALUE

Depreciation

Vehicles can lose value over time and as mileage increases. Estimated depreciation can therefore be considered when calculating the broader cost of using a vehicle.

06 / ROUTE

Tolls and journey charges

Depending on the route, tolls and other journey-specific charges can add directly to the cost of travelling.

Step 4: See the bigger picture

Instead of seeing only a fuel figure, the result can show the estimated contribution of different costs to the journey.

For example, a journey might look inexpensive at the fuel pump while having a significantly higher overall cost once vehicle-related costs are considered.

The result can be expressed as:

Fuel + Maintenance + Tyres + Wear + Depreciation + Journey Costs = True Travel Cost

Why use an estimate?

Some vehicle costs can be measured precisely. Others cannot.

Your actual fuel consumption will depend on traffic, weather, driving style and the vehicle's condition. Maintenance costs vary. Tyre life varies. Future resale values cannot be known with certainty.

Estimates, not guarantees. TrueTravelCost uses estimates and assumptions rather than presenting uncertain figures as guaranteed costs. The aim is to provide a useful comparison and a broader understanding of vehicle costs.

One journey or hundreds?

The same calculation can be useful for a single trip or for journeys you repeat regularly.

A daily commute, for example, can be converted into weekly, monthly or annual costs.
